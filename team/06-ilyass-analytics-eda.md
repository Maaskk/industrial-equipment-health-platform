# Ilyass - Analytics, EDA, and Business Evidence

GitHub: pending

Branch: `feature/ilyass`

## Mission

Turn the dataset and model results into understandable business evidence.

## Main Deliverables

- Exploratory data analysis.
- Dataset summary:
  - number of engines
  - cycles
  - sensor columns
  - missing values
  - target distribution
- Business KPIs:
  - predicted high-risk equipment count
  - average remaining useful life
  - failure-risk segments
- Charts for the report and presentation.
- Explanation of why predictive maintenance creates value.

## Acceptance Criteria

- EDA results are reproducible.
- Charts are readable and directly useful for the final presentation.
- Analysis links model outputs to business value.
- At least three strong visuals are ready for the final report.

## First Tasks

1. Profile the selected dataset.
2. Identify useful sensor patterns.
3. Create lifecycle and failure-risk charts.
4. Write business interpretation notes.
5. Provide demo scenarios to Ossama and Hajar.
